Executive summary

Kastros HR is a secure, browser-based human resources platform built for Kastros Group. It centralises employee records, time off, expenses, compliance documents, learning, performance data, and hiring in one system — with role-based access for employees, HR administrators, and the CEO.

What leadership gains: Real-time visibility of headcount, leave pipeline, open recruitment, training compliance, and probation milestones — without spreadsheets or disconnected tools. HR gains a single source of truth for profiles, letters, ID cards, and audit history.
  • Deployed as a modern web application (cloud-ready, Firebase authentication and data storage).
  • Multi–business unit support: UAE, Karachi, Multan — with currency defaults (AED / PKR).
  • End-to-end employee lifecycle: public job applications → HR hire → onboarding → daily self-service → offboarding data.
  • Built-in compliance: policy acknowledgements, Conflict of Interest (CoI) workflow, document library, audit log.
  • Employee enablement: public how-to manual and in-app help — no login required for training material.

1. Platform & security

Technology foundation

  • Application: Next.js 15 web application — responsive for desktop and tablet use.
  • Authentication: Firebase Auth (work email + password); role enforced via secure session and custom claims (Employee, HR Admin, CEO).
  • Data: Firestore (production) with structured HR store — employees, leave, expenses, documents, training, cases, audit, etc.
  • File storage: Firebase Storage (and local fallback for development) for CVs, receipts, profile photos, policy files, CoI submissions.
  • Hosting: Designed for Vercel or equivalent cloud deployment with environment-based configuration.

Security controls

  • Route-level access control — users only see modules permitted for their role.
  • File downloads authorised per document type (own files, manager scope, or HR-wide).
  • Audit trail of material actions (who changed what and when).
  • CEO-only role promotion — prevents unauthorised elevation to HR Admin.
  • Password reset via Firebase — onboarding emails for new hires when mail is configured.

2. User roles & access

Role Typical users Access scope
Employee All staff Own overview, leave, expenses, documents (policies & CoI), training, own performance reviews, org chart (view). Cannot access other employees' HR records or admin setup.
HR Admin People Operations Full HR module set: People directory, onboarding, recruiting, settings, organization, letters, cases, approvals, compensation entry, document management.
CEO Executive leadership Same as HR Admin plus executive dashboard (company-wide KPIs), leave sign-off where required (Pending CEO), and Firebase role management.

3. Complete feature catalogue

The platform is organised into the following modules (sidebar groups in the application).

Overview

  • Dashboard (Overview): Role-specific home — employees see profile summary, leave balance estimate, goals count, quick links; HR/CEO see headcount, pending leave, jobs, cases, probation alerts; CEO sees additional company pulse metrics.
  • My leave: Leave balances by type and year; submit requests; track approval status; HR manages entitlements and approves/denies.
  • Expense claims: Submit claims with category, amount, currency, receipt; HR approves, rejects, marks reimbursed (Paid).

People

  • People (directory): Searchable roster; full employee profiles; inline edit for HR; CNIC expiry alerts.
  • Onboarding: Add team member — creates employee + Firebase login; comprehensive intake form.
  • Recruiting: Job postings; applicant pipeline; public apply link; CV and attachment review.
  • Transfer / Posting: Record cross–business unit and department moves with effective dates.
  • Reporting channel: Live org chart from “Reports to” manager email on each profile.

Time & performance

  • Performance: Formal review records (cycle, grade, criteria, comments); HR/CEO record reviews; employees view own history.
  • Learning (training): Assign training with due dates; internal/external provider; upload materials (PDF/PPT); track Required/Done and attendance.

Letters & documents

  • Letters: HR letter repository (promotion, termination, etc.); printable views; appointment letter quick-generate from profile.
  • Documents: Company library; policy manual acknowledgements; CoI template upload and employee signed submissions; register personnel files.
  • HR cases: Confidential case log for investigations and employee relations matters.

Setup & administration

  • Organization setup: Business units, departments, sub-departments, job descriptions.
  • Settings: Leave policy (types and default days); salary allowance catalog; demo reset (non-production).
  • Security: Audit log viewer for compliance spot-checks.

External & support surfaces

  • Public careers portal: /apply/[jobId] — candidates apply without login.
  • Training manual (public): /training-manual/how-to — Employee vs HR/CEO guides with search; no login.
  • In-app help: Floating help button opens manual in new tab while using the system.

4. Employee experience (self-service)

Every employee with a work email and profile can:

  • Sign in securely and set password via HR-triggered reset email.
  • View personal dashboard: job title, department, manager, joining date, probation reminder, estimated annual leave remaining.
  • Request leave with type, date range, and notes; follow status through HR/CEO approval chain.
  • Submit expense claims with receipt attachment and currency selection (AED, PKR, USD).
  • Acknowledge company policies from the policy manual with timestamped record.
  • Download CoI template, sign offline, upload signed declaration.
  • View assigned training, open materials, complete compliance programmes.
  • View own performance review history.
  • View reporting channel to understand team structure.
  • Use public how-to manual (employee path) for step-by-step guidance.

5. HR & CEO operations (detailed)

People & profiles

HR maintains a rich employee record including:

  • Identity: name, salutation, gender, DOB, nationality, marital status, religion, CNIC, address
  • Employment: title, department, sub-department, location, BU, type, status, joining, probation
  • Reports to (manager email), employee ID for card
  • Phones, emergency contact, family/COI declaration
  • Vehicle & licence details where applicable
  • Statutory flags: Gratuity, EOBI, Provident Fund
  • Profile photo (ID card)
  • Education & certification records with files
  • Personnel documents linked to individual
  • Policy acknowledgement on behalf of employee when needed

HR actions on profiles: Edit profile (inline), resend password reset, generate appointment letter, print corporate ID card, manage salary/compensation with configurable allowances, add/remove academic and document records.

Leave administration

  • Configure leave types and standard days per year in Settings.
  • Apply defaults to all employees for a calendar year.
  • Override per-employee entitlements on Leave page.
  • Approve or deny requests; Pending HR → Pending CEO → Approved/Denied workflow.
  • Company-wide pending count on executive dashboard.

Expense administration

  • Review all employee claims with receipt preview.
  • Approve or reject with audit trail.
  • Mark Approved claims as Paid when finance completes reimbursement.
  • Dashboard counts for pending review and approved-awaiting-payment.

Onboarding & offboarding data

  • Single flow creates roster entry and Firebase account.
  • Recommended top-down hire order for correct org chart.
  • Email change on profile can sync to authentication (identity update).
  • Employee status: Active, On leave, Offboarding, Separated.

6. Talent acquisition

  • Job management: Create roles with description; share public application URL.
  • Applicant tracking: Table of candidates per job; status; expand full application (education rows, cover letter, LinkedIn, experience).
  • Attachments: CV, education certificate, other certs — stored and viewable by HR.
  • Decisions: Approve or reject applications.
  • Hire path: Approved candidates onboarded via HR form (internal fields: department, manager, joining date, probation, BU — not collected on public form).
  • LinkedIn kit: Supporting collateral for external job posts (where configured in UI).

7. Compensation & benefits data

  • Per-employee compensation (HR/CEO only on People profile): gross salary, basic salary, currency, multiple allowance lines.
  • Allowance catalog in Settings: configurable types (e.g. fuel in liters, housing allowance) — HR defines catalog, applies per employee.
  • Appointment letter can pull saved gross salary when generating letters.
  • Statutory flags on profile (Gratuity, EOBI, PF) support letter generation and local compliance context.
  • Display formatting with thousands separators for clarity.

8. Compliance & documents

  • Policy manual: Versioned policies; employees acknowledge in-app; HR can record acknowledgement for paper sign-offs.
  • Company library: HR-published notices and templates visible organisation-wide; preview and print.
  • Personnel files: Contracts, visas, certificates — linked to named employee, not mixed into company library.
  • CoI programme: HR uploads master template; employees submit signed copies; HR reviews registry.
  • File security: Authorised download only — linked to employee, document, application, training, or expense receipt.

9. Governance, audit & support

Audit & accountability

  • Security module exposes chronological audit log (actor, action description, timestamp).
  • Material HR mutations logged: onboarding, leave decisions, expense status, document registration, role changes, etc.

Executive controls (CEO)

  • Company pulse dashboard: active headcount, on leave, departments, pending leave company-wide, open jobs, new applications, open training.
  • Role manager: promote/demote Employee ↔ HR Admin ↔ CEO in Firebase (effective after re-login).
  • Probation alerts: employees within 10 days of probation end — proactive HR action.

Training & adoption

  • Public manual with Employee / HR/CEO paths and search — shareable by email without credentials.
  • Direct links: ?role=employee or ?role=hr for targeted comms.
  • In-app ? help button on every authenticated page.
